# Case Study Review: AI Portfolio Reviewer for Product Designers

An AI-powered portfolio case study reviewer for product designers. Use this single-file AI skill to get structured design feedback, scores across ten dimensions, and actionable improvements for one UX or product design portfolio case study at a time.

I built this working framework to get feedback I could act on while working on my own portfolio. It reviews the decisions behind the work, visual craft, writing, desktop and mobile layouts, and motion where it can be inspected. Staff Product Designer is the suggested review level, and you can change it to match your target role.

## Use it

1. Download [SKILL.md](https://github.com/twjulia/case-study-review/releases/latest/download/SKILL.md).
2. Attach the file to an AI conversation and share the direct link to **one case study**. Screenshots or a PDF also work when the page is unavailable.
3. Paste this request:

> Use the attached Case Study Review skill (SKILL.md) to review this case study: [case-study URL].
>
> Evaluate it against the Staff Product Designer bar. Review the desktop and mobile experience, including motion where available. Score it across all ten dimensions, show evidence for the scores, and give me the five most important fixes.
>
> Review only this case study. Do not edit my website.

**Staff Product Designer is the suggested starting point.** Change the target level or share a job description for a more tailored review.

The AI needs access to the material it evaluates. Screenshots support a visual review; motion needs a live page or recording. The review should state anything it could not inspect.

## What you get

- A scorecard across ten dimensions, with reasons for each score.
- Specific evidence and suggestions for revision.
- Two or three panel questions grounded in the case study.
- Up to five changes to make first, ranked by priority.

Scores guide revision. They do not predict hiring outcomes.

The skill is a single file. No other files or setup are needed.
